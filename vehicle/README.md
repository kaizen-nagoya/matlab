# Q1 Please indicate the number of parts in a four-wheeled passenger car and provide a complete list of all parts.
A1 by ChatGPT
A modern four-wheeled passenger car contains **thousands to tens of thousands of individual parts**, depending on how you count:

* **~2,000–4,000 major assemblies/components** (modules)
* **~10,000–30,000 discrete parts** including bolts, clips, seals, fasteners
* **EVs often fewer moving powertrain parts**, but more electronics
* **Luxury cars** often have more parts than economy cars

There is **no single universal “complete list”** because it depends on:

* ICE / Hybrid / EV
* Sedan / SUV / Truck
* OEM design philosophy
* Market regulations
* Optional equipment

So the practical way is a **full hierarchical bill of materials (BOM)**. Below is a comprehensive automotive engineering master list.
